Mariatta Wijaya is a multi-award-winning open source maintainer and
contributor, and a Python core developer. Her focus in CPython is the core
workflow and the documentation: the tooling, the automation, and the guides
that make it easier for other people to contribute and for maintainers to keep
up with the work.

She speaks at conferences around the world, and much of her work is in building
the spaces where that work happens: she runs PyLadies Vancouver, organizes
conferences including PyCascades, and co-organizes the Maintainer Summit at
PyCon US.
