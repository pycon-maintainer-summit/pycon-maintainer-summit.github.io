## Our mission

The PyCon US Maintainers Summit is a dedicated space for the people who
keep Python's open source ecosystem running. We come together to share
what we've learned, find collaborators, and support each other in the
often-invisible work of maintenance.

The summit is organized in collaboration with the Python Software
Foundation and PyCon US.

## The team

The summit is run entirely by volunteers. Meet [this year's and past
organizers](/organizers/).
