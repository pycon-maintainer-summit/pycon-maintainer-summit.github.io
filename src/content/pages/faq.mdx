<FAQ>

<FAQItem q="Do I need a separate ticket for the summit?">
No. The Maintainer Summit is part of PyCon US, and there is no extra charge
on top of your PyCon US registration. Space in the room is limited, so
arriving early on the day helps. See [this year's summit](/events/) for the
room and schedule.
</FAQItem>

<FAQItem q="Who is the summit for?">
Maintainers of open source Python projects, big and small, new and veteran.
If you keep a project running, review its pull requests, cut its releases, or
answer its issues, this is a room full of people doing the same thing. You do
not need to maintain a well-known project to belong here.
</FAQItem>

<FAQItem q="What actually happens during the day?">
A mix of prepared talks, lightning talks, and facilitated roundtable
discussions. The blend changes every year. The
[event formats guide](/docs/event-formats/) describes each session type and
how we plan them.
</FAQItem>

<FAQItem q="How do I propose a talk?">
Talks are selected from a Call for Proposals that opens ahead of each summit.
The [CFP guide](/docs/cfp-guide/) covers what we look for and how proposals
are reviewed; watch [the news feed](/news/) for the announcement when the
next CFP opens.
</FAQItem>

<FAQItem q="Are the talks recorded?">
No. The summit does not record its sessions, so being in the room is the way
to catch them. The [talk archive](/talks/) lists every session from every
year, and where a speaker has shared their own slides we link them there.
</FAQItem>

<FAQItem q="Is there a code of conduct?">
Yes. The summit follows the PyCon US Code of Conduct during the conference
days, and a community-run code of conduct in the unofficial Discord. Both,
along with how to report a concern, are on the
[code of conduct page](/docs/code-of-conduct/).
</FAQItem>

<FAQItem q="Can I help organize?">
Yes, and we would like that. The summit is run entirely by volunteers. Email
us at [maintainers-pyconus@googlegroups.com](mailto:maintainers-pyconus@googlegroups.com)
or see the [contact page](/contact/) for the other ways to reach the team.
</FAQItem>

</FAQ>
