The 2020 Maintainers Summit was planned as a one-day in-person event on
**Friday, April 17, 2020** at the David L. Lawrence Convention Center in
Pittsburgh. PyCon US 2020 was cancelled in person and replaced with an
online edition due to the COVID-19 pandemic; the summit's full agenda was
not published on the archive page.

The planned format included a panel discussion, two lightning-talk
sessions, and an open discussion. Speakers and individual talk titles
were not preserved on the archive page.

## Planned schedule

- 11:00–11:10 — Welcome to Maintainers Summit
- 11:10–12:00 — Panel discussion
- 12:00–12:40 — Lightning talks
- 12:40–1:40 — Lunch
- 1:40–2:30 — Lightning talks
- 2:30–4:00 — Open discussion
