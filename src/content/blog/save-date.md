Your 2027 PyCon US Maintainer Summit organizers just had their first planning meeting, and we're very excited to say "Save the Date!" for **Monday, November 16, 2026**.

That's the day our **Call for Proposals** opens, so please mark your calendars! Here are a few links to help you remember:

- [Add to Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r/eventedit?action=TEMPLATE&text=PyCon+US+Maintainer+Summit+2027+Announcement&dates=20261116/20261117&details=Time+to+check+out+the+PyCon+US+Maintainer+Summit+website+at+https://pycon-maintainer-summit.github.io/)
- [Add to Outlook / Office 365](https://outlook.office.com/calendar/0/deeplink/compose?path=/calendar/action/compose&rru=addevent&allday=true&subject=PyCon%20US%20Maintainer%20Summit%202027%20Announcement&startdt=2026-11-16&enddt=2026-11-17&body=Time%20to%20check%20out%20the%20PyCon%20US%20Maintainer%20Summit%20website%20at%20https%3A%2F%2Fpycon-maintainer-summit.github.io%2F)
- [Subscribe to our calendar feed (.ics)](/events/calendar.ics), which includes this and every other important date for the Summit

You can also keep an eye on the [2027 Summit page](/events/2027-pycon-us/), where we'll post all the key dates as they're confirmed.
