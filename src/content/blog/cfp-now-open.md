We invite you to speak at the 2027 PyCon US Maintainer Summit, on
**Saturday, May 15, 2027**, in Long Beach, California. Whether you maintain a
project, contribute to one, or support the people who do, we would love to hear
from you. [Submit a proposal](https://maintainer-summit.us.pycon.org/speakers/propose/)
by **January 16, 2027**.

## What to propose

We welcome two kinds of session:

- **Talk:** 15 minutes.
- **Lightning talk:** 5 minutes, for the Tools Track: news about a project,
  tool, or initiative in the Python ecosystem.

We invite proposals on any topic about maintaining an open source Python
project. We especially encourage first-time speakers and people from groups
underrepresented in tech to submit. You do not need to maintain a large project
to have something worth sharing. The [Call for Proposals guide](/docs/cfp-guide/)
has topic ideas, a walkthrough of the form, and how we choose. For
inspiration, see the [talks presented at past summits](/talks/).

## The main PyCon US CFP

The Maintainer Summit has its own call for proposals, separate from the main
PyCon US call for proposals. Each has its own limit of three proposals, and
proposals to the summit do not count toward the limit of the main PyCon US CFP.
You are welcome to submit to both, even the same proposal.

## Important dates

- Call for proposals closes: January 16, 2027
- Speaker notifications: February 1, 2027
- PyCon US travel grant applications: December 8, 2026 to February 19, 2027

Sessions are in person, and acceptance does not include funding to attend. If
you need help with the cost, apply early for a PyCon US travel grant.

Questions? Email us at maintainer-pyconus@googlegroups.com. We look forward to
reading your proposals!
