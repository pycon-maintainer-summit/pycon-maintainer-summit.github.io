Thank you for using the Maintainer Summit's web app.

The web app at <https://maintainer-summit.us.pycon.org/> is the PyCon US
Maintainer Summit's instance of [Flashlights](https://github.com/mariatta/flashlights),
the open source software the summit's organizers use to run the call for
proposals and the program.

The Maintainer Summit is part of PyCon US, and the web app is hosted on the
Python Software Foundation's infrastructure, except for the storage bucket
that holds uploaded files, which is the summit's own. We follow the same
[policies](https://policies.python.org/) as the PSF, including its
[privacy policy](https://www.python.org/privacy/).

By creating an account you agree to these terms, to the summit's
[acceptable use policy](/docs/acceptable-use-policy/) and to its
[code of conduct](/docs/code-of-conduct/).

## Your account

You are responsible for what is done with your account. Keep your password
to yourself. The web app can also sign you in with a one-time code sent to
your email address; that code is yours alone, and nobody from the summit
will ever ask you for it. Tell us at <maintainer-pyconus@googlegroups.com>
if you believe your account has been used by someone else.

Your email address has to be verified before you can use the account. An
account whose address is never verified is deleted after seven days.

## Your information

We keep the information you give us (your profile, your proposal, your
volunteer application, the files you upload) to run the summit, and we share
it only with the organizers who need it for that. Sponsors are recorded by
the organizers and are not shown your details. The web app holds no
registration or ticket information: attendees register through PyCon US.

Some of what speakers enter is meant to be public. Once a session is
accepted and published, the speaker's name appears with it, and their bio,
photo and links appear on the summit's website. A setting on the speaker
profile hides the bio, photo and links; the name still appears on the
session. A Discord username given for the summit's chat is never shown
publicly.

You can edit your profile yourself. To remove your account, or anything
you cannot change yourself, ask us at <maintainer-pyconus@googlegroups.com>.

## Where your information goes

The web app serves every page itself. It runs no analytics or advertising,
sets no third-party cookies, and loads no scripts or fonts from other
companies, so using it tells no one else what you do there. The only cookies
are the web app's own, which keep you signed in and protect its forms. The
challenge on the sign-up form is generated on the web app's own server, not
by an outside service.

Your information leaves the web app only through the services that run it:

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Cabotage, the Python Software Foundation's hosting platform | runs the app, its database and its task queue | everything the web app stores, apart from uploaded files |
| DigitalOcean Spaces, a bucket the summit pays for in its own account | keeps the files people upload | headshots and logos. Public ones are shown from the bucket's address, so your browser fetches those images from DigitalOcean |
| Mailgun, through the Python Software Foundation's account | sends the web app's email | each email the web app sends: the recipient's address, the subject and the body. The PSF's staff administer the account; the summit's organizers have no access to it |

The web app does not report errors to any outside service. If the summit
turns that on, this page will say so first.

The web app keeps a record of each email it sends for a year after the
edition it belongs to, then deletes it.

Everything the web app does is public code: Flashlights itself, and the
summit's own additions and deployment in
[pycon-maintainer-summit/flashlights](https://github.com/pycon-maintainer-summit/flashlights).

## Our side

The organizers may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms, the
acceptable use policy or the code of conduct, and may remove content that
does. We may change these terms; changes are announced on this site and by
email to accounts that are active at the time.

Questions about these terms go to <maintainer-pyconus@googlegroups.com>.
