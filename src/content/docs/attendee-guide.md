## Before the summit

**Keep the dates in your calendar.** Each edition's page lists its
important dates, with buttons to subscribe to them in Apple Calendar,
Outlook, Thunderbird or Google Calendar. New dates appear in your
calendar as we add them, so you only subscribe once. The
[.ics file](/events/calendar.ics) is there too for any other calendar app.
